About Me

Get to know Jordan

I've always loved stories — the kind a song tells, the kind a logo hints at, the kind a well-placed sentence carries. When we started Bloom, I wanted it to feel less like an app and more like a club: somewhere teens actually want to hang out and help each other.

My job was the "feel" of everything — the taglines, the color choices, the little moments that make you smile. I'm a big believer that warmth isn't decoration; it's the whole point. If a shy 14-year-old opens Bloom and thinks "this seems nice," I did my job.

I also make beats in my bedroom, help friends polish their first resumes, and run a tiny zine with two classmates. Every project I touch is about giving teens a voice.
