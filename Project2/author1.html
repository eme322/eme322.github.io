About Me

A little more about Maya

I started coding when I was 13 after a teacher showed me how to make a simple webpage. What hooked me wasn't the syntax — it was seeing something I built appear on a screen. That same spark is why I helped create Bloom: I wanted every teen to feel that "I made this" moment.

I care about design that's warm and welcoming, not cold or intimidating. I believe good interfaces should feel like a friend explaining things, not a manual. That philosophy shaped every button and card on Bloom.

Outside of building Bloom, I run a small study group at school where I help friends with their first HTML and CSS projects. Watching someone go from "I can't code" to "look what I made" never gets old.
