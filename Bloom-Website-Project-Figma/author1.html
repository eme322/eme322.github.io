About Me

Hey! I'm Alex — a developer, problem-solver, and lifelong learner. I started coding at 13 after realizing I could build literally anything I imagined if I just learned the right tools. Since then, I've built apps, entered hackathons, and co-founded Bloom alongside my partner Jordan.

Outside of code, I'm into basketball, lo-fi music, and teaching younger kids at my local library's coding club. I believe the best software is built by people who genuinely care about the humans using it.
