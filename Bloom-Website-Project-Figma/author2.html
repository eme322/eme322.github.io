About Me

Hi, I'm Jordan! Design is how I make sense of the world. Whether I'm sketching in a notebook, crafting a brand system in Figma, or animating a logo in Procreate, I'm always asking: “How does this make someone feel?”

I co-founded Bloom because I wanted a place where teens could share their gifts without money getting in the way. I handle everything visual — from the logo to the match cards you see on the home page. I'm also part of my school's Yearbook club and run an illustration account with 2K+ followers.
